Make props and strings behave the same in every component. Attributes and event handlers a component doesn't name reach its main element: a consumer's handler runs before the component's own, and `preventDefault()` in it cancels the component's behavior (`IconButton`, `Menu.Trigger`, `Popover.Trigger`, `Sheet.Close`, `DateEntry`, `Rating`, `NavigationMenu` parts and others no longer drop or replace them); `ThemeProvider` keeps the consumer's `style` and `Chip` its attributes. Every component with a value or an open state takes `default<Name>` and reports changes through `on<Name>Change` (`PasswordField` gets `onValueChange`, `defaultVisible` and `onVisibleChange`, `Pagination` `defaultPageSize`); `Select.Root` gets `open`, `defaultOpen` and `onOpenChange`, and `Menu.Root` and `Tooltip.Root` get `defaultOpen`. The standard button, pager and date and time entry labels moved into `Localizations` (`back`, `ok`, `cancel`, `previousPage`, `dateHelp`, `today`, ...), so the `strings` of `LocaleProvider` replace them too; the search button and field read "Pesquisa" in Portuguese, as in Flutter. New strings name the color format chooser of `ColorPicker`, the `Steps` buttons, a `CodeBlock` without a `label` (now always a named region) and the zoom slider of `ImageCropper`. `Checkbox`, `Switch`, `RadioGroup` and `Slider` take `form` and submit nothing while disabled. An invalid `accent` falls back to the default one with a warning instead of failing to render, and `ColorDisk` shows an invalid color as an empty disk.
