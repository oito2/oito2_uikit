Add the overlays, pickers and shortcut components. `@oito2/ui` gets `Popover` with `PopoverController`, `HoverCard`, `ContextMenu` (with the `Menu` items), `Sheet` from either side or the bottom with snap points, `Toaster` with `toast()`, `toast.promise()` and `ToastHandle`, `FloatingPanel`, `Tour` with `TourController`, `Calendar` and `DatePicker` (single date or range, ISO week numbers), `ColorPicker` and `ColorPickerButton` (hex, RGB and HSL fields, opacity, swatches), `ShortcutLabel`, `ShortcutRecorder` and `CommandPalette` with `openCommandPalette()`. `DateEntry` gains a button that opens a calendar (`pickerButton`).
