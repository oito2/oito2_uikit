Fix accessibility and API gaps found while building the showcase pages. `ScrollArea` takes the keyboard focus while its content scrolls, and the `Carousel` slides take it instead of the carousel root, so both scroll from the keyboard. `Steps` names each step from its visible title and description. `PageIndicator` gets a `label` for its group of dots and `Avatar` a `label` for an avatar without a name. Read-only and disabled `Rating` and `AngleSlider` stay sliders with `aria-readonly` or `aria-disabled` and their value in `aria-valuetext`, so an `aria-label` names them; a read-only `AngleSlider` is no longer dimmed. `Tooltip.Trigger` without `child` opens from the keyboard and describes the focused element inside it. `TitleBar` and `HeaderBar` get `headingLevel`. `AlertDialog` labels its buttons with the localized "OK" and "Cancel" by default. `EditableLabel` opens filled and focused when `editing` is set from outside. `Rating` and `AngleSlider` get `defaultValue` and `Pagination` `defaultPage`. `Carousel.Root` exposes its `CarouselController` through a bindable `controller`. `Command.icon` is an `IconComponent`. A composed `Select` shows the label of its value before it first opens. The `WatermarkAlignment` and `PaneSide` types are exported.
