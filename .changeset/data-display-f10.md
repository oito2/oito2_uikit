Add the data, display and icon components. `@oito2/ui` gets `ListBox` (`Root`, `Item`, `Group`, `GroupLabel`, `Empty`) over a `ListCollection` and a `SelectionController`, `TreeView` with `TreeController` (virtualized rows, tristate check boxes, filtering, inline rename with F2 and lazy loading of children), `JsonTreeView` with `jsonTree()`, `Avatar` and `AvatarGroup` with `initials()`, `Marquee`, `TimerController` with `TimerText` and `formatDuration()`, `AnimatedIcon` with `animatedIcons`, `PlaceholderIcon`, `DiagonalClip`, the `draggable` and `undershoot` attachments and the `pageTransition` transition. The new `@oito2/ui/icons` entry re-exports every Lucide icon and adds `freedesktopIcon()`, which maps freedesktop.org icon names to Lucide icons.
