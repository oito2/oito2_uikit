Add the advanced controls and entries. `@oito2/ui` gets `SplitButton`, `OptionButton`, `ColorDisk`, `Swap`, `ToggleGroup`, `SegmentGroup`, `ChoiceChipBar`, `SearchField`, `SearchTitleField`, `PasswordField` with a Caps Lock warning, `CopyButton` and `CopyField`, `HighlightedText`, `EditableLabel`, `PinEntry`, `TagsField` with suggestions, `Combobox` (also exported as `Autocomplete`), `DateEntry`, `TimeEntry` and `DateTimeEntry` that read and write the locale's formats, `SegmentedEntry`, `NumberEntry` with the wheel and scrubbing, `Rating` and `AngleSlider`, with their parsing helpers (`parseCompactDate`, `parseTime`, `parseNumber`, `highlightRanges`…). `Chip` takes `showCheckmark`.
