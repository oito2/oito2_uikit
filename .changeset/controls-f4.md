Add the basic controls, styled like the oito2_ui Material 3 theme with pointer and touch sizes: `Button` (filled, tonal, outlined, text and elevated), `IconButton` (with a tooltip and a selected state), `BackButton`, `CloseButton` and `Fab`; `Checkbox` (with a mixed state), `Switch` (with on/off shapes), `RadioGroup`, `ToggleButton`, `CheckButton`, `RadioButton`, `SwitchButton` and the `CheckboxListTile`, `RadioListTile` and `SwitchListTile` rows; `ListTile`; `CircularProgress` and `LinearProgress`; `Slider` (one or two thumbs, divisions, marks, vertical); `Card`, `Chip`, `Badge` and `Separator`; `Field` with `TextField` and `TextArea`; `Tooltip`, `Dialog`, `AlertDialog`, `Menu` (with check box and radio items and submenus) and `Select`; and `ScrollArea`. Themes now also set `--oito2-color-selected-fill`, `--oito2-color-on-selected-fill`, `--oito2-color-on-primary-text` and `--oito2-color-off-thumb`, and `componentColors` returns them.
