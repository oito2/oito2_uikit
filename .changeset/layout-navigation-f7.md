Add the layout and navigation components. `@oito2/ui` gets `BorderContainer`, `Section`, `Tile` and `TileList`, `Watermark`, `SelectableContainer`, `Banner`, `InfoBox` and `InfoBadge`, `Expandable` and `Accordion` (also exported as `ExpansionPanel`), `Tabs` in the pill and subtle styles, in a row or a column, with a `tabs` slot on `HeaderBar`, `NavigationRail` in three styles, `NavigationBar`, `NavigationPage`, which switches between a rail and a bottom bar by width and input, `MasterDetail`, `PanedView` and `Splitter` with keyboard-resizable dividers, `Steps`, `Pagination`, `PageIndicator`, `Carousel`, `TableOfContents` with scroll-spy and `PageController`. Every color theme now also defines the message colors (`--oito2-color-info-box-<type>`, `--oito2-color-info-badge-text-<type>`…) and `--oito2-color-on-primary-tint`, and the presets of `@oito2/ui-themes` include them.
