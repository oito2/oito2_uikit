Add the extra components. `@oito2/ui-extras` gets `QrCode` (SVG, error levels, rounded modules, a center image) with `qrModules()`, `SignaturePad` with `SignatureController` (speed- and pressure-sensitive ink, undo, clear, SVG, canvas and PNG export), `ImageCropper`, `CropController` and `CropPreview` (zoom, pan, quarter turns, flip, circle crop, keyboard and wheel control) and `NavigationMenu` (`Root`, `List`, `Item`, `Trigger`, `Link`, `Content`, `Indicator`), a header menu with flyout panels.
