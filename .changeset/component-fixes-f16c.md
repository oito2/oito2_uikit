Fix keyboard, accessibility and behavior gaps across the components. Inputs: `Slider` values come without floating-point noise and `onValueCommit` fires only after a change; `NumberEntry` lets Enter submit the form; `DateEntry` reads dates in locales with their own digits; `Calendar` keeps focus when a month button turns disabled; `SegmentedEntry` spreads a pasted value over its segments; a disabled `TagsField` is out of the Tab order; `EditableLabel` starts editing with Space; `PinEntry` keeps the caret at the end; time and date entries reformat when the locale changes; `ChoiceChipBar` updates its arrows when chips change. Layout and data: `TreeView` and `ListBox` show a focus ring when reached with Tab, and `TreeView` expands all siblings with `*`; `Carousel` gets `Carousel.PlayPause` and `playing`/`defaultPlaying`/`onPlayingChange`, stops rotating while the window is hidden and announces slides only while still; `Marquee` gets `paused`/`defaultPaused`/`onPausedChange`; `Splitter` reports sizes on resize and reapplies changed limits; an overflowing `ScrollArea` is a named region; `PanedView`, `ListBox.Group`, `TableOfContents` (focus and hash), `NavigationPage` density and `jsonTree` cycles are fixed. Overlays: modal dialogs and sheets make the rest of the page inert; danger toasts are alerts, toasts don't expire while the window is hidden and `Toaster` gets its own `maxVisible`; `Popover.Content` takes `role`; hover cards keep other `aria-describedby` ids and no longer reopen on blur; touch long presses tolerate small movements; `Tour`, `Combobox` and `Select` do less work. Extras: `SignatureController.toSvg` escapes the color, `ImageCropper` takes `crossOrigin` and pinches around the fingers, `NavigationMenu` panels are groups, and `SignaturePad` paints incrementally. `CodeBlock` no longer numbers a line after a trailing newline.
