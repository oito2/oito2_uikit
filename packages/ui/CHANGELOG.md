# @oito2/ui

## 0.1.0

### Minor Changes

- 84e5df9: Add the oito2 color system. `@oito2/ui` derives complete schemes (all Material 3 roles plus text-safe semantic, chrome and syntax colors, WCAG AA or AAA with high contrast) from an accent with `createColorTheme` or from a theme family's roles with `colorThemeFromRoles`, applies them with `ThemeProvider` (light, dark or system; accent and high contrast from the system by default) and exposes them to components through `useTheme()`. The Svelte-free parts are also available from `@oito2/ui/theme`, and `@oito2/ui/schemes.css` ships every base scheme as CSS. `@oito2/ui-themes` adds the 13 presets (Everforest, Rosé Pine, Nord, Dracula/Alucard and Catppuccin) with `presetColorTheme`, `presetTheme` and `@oito2/ui-themes/presets.css`.
- e1526eb: Fix accessibility and API gaps found while building the showcase pages. `ScrollArea` takes the keyboard focus while its content scrolls, and the `Carousel` slides take it instead of the carousel root, so both scroll from the keyboard. `Steps` names each step from its visible title and description. `PageIndicator` gets a `label` for its group of dots and `Avatar` a `label` for an avatar without a name. Read-only and disabled `Rating` and `AngleSlider` stay sliders with `aria-readonly` or `aria-disabled` and their value in `aria-valuetext`, so an `aria-label` names them; a read-only `AngleSlider` is no longer dimmed. `Tooltip.Trigger` without `child` opens from the keyboard and describes the focused element inside it. `TitleBar` and `HeaderBar` get `headingLevel`. `AlertDialog` labels its buttons with the localized "OK" and "Cancel" by default. `EditableLabel` opens filled and focused when `editing` is set from outside. `Rating` and `AngleSlider` get `defaultValue` and `Pagination` `defaultPage`. `Carousel.Root` exposes its `CarouselController` through a bindable `controller`. `Command.icon` is an `IconComponent`. A composed `Select` shows the label of its value before it first opens. The `WatermarkAlignment` and `PaneSide` types are exported.
- 4cbafa7: Fix keyboard, accessibility and behavior gaps across the components. Inputs: `Slider` values come without floating-point noise and `onValueCommit` fires only after a change; `NumberEntry` lets Enter submit the form; `DateEntry` reads dates in locales with their own digits; `Calendar` keeps focus when a month button turns disabled; `SegmentedEntry` spreads a pasted value over its segments; a disabled `TagsField` is out of the Tab order; `EditableLabel` starts editing with Space; `PinEntry` keeps the caret at the end; time and date entries reformat when the locale changes; `ChoiceChipBar` updates its arrows when chips change. Layout and data: `TreeView` and `ListBox` show a focus ring when reached with Tab, and `TreeView` expands all siblings with `*`; `Carousel` gets `Carousel.PlayPause` and `playing`/`defaultPlaying`/`onPlayingChange`, stops rotating while the window is hidden and announces slides only while still; `Marquee` gets `paused`/`defaultPaused`/`onPausedChange`; `Splitter` reports sizes on resize and reapplies changed limits; an overflowing `ScrollArea` is a named region; `PanedView`, `ListBox.Group`, `TableOfContents` (focus and hash), `NavigationPage` density and `jsonTree` cycles are fixed. Overlays: modal dialogs and sheets make the rest of the page inert; danger toasts are alerts, toasts don't expire while the window is hidden and `Toaster` gets its own `maxVisible`; `Popover.Content` takes `role`; hover cards keep other `aria-describedby` ids and no longer reopen on blur; touch long presses tolerate small movements; `Tour`, `Combobox` and `Select` do less work. Extras: `SignatureController.toSvg` escapes the color, `ImageCropper` takes `crossOrigin` and pinches around the fingers, `NavigationMenu` panels are groups, and `SignaturePad` paints incrementally. `CodeBlock` no longer numbers a line after a trailing newline.
- 540853e: Add the advanced controls and entries. `@oito2/ui` gets `SplitButton`, `OptionButton`, `ColorDisk`, `Swap`, `ToggleGroup`, `SegmentGroup`, `ChoiceChipBar`, `SearchField`, `SearchTitleField`, `PasswordField` with a Caps Lock warning, `CopyButton` and `CopyField`, `HighlightedText`, `EditableLabel`, `PinEntry`, `TagsField` with suggestions, `Combobox` (also exported as `Autocomplete`), `DateEntry`, `TimeEntry` and `DateTimeEntry` that read and write the locale's formats, `SegmentedEntry`, `NumberEntry` with the wheel and scrubbing, `Rating` and `AngleSlider`, with their parsing helpers (`parseCompactDate`, `parseTime`, `parseNumber`, `highlightRanges`…). `Chip` takes `showCheckmark`.
- bfc458c: Add the basic controls, styled like the oito2_ui Material 3 theme with pointer and touch sizes: `Button` (filled, tonal, outlined, text and elevated), `IconButton` (with a tooltip and a selected state), `BackButton`, `CloseButton` and `Fab`; `Checkbox` (with a mixed state), `Switch` (with on/off shapes), `RadioGroup`, `ToggleButton`, `CheckButton`, `RadioButton`, `SwitchButton` and the `CheckboxListTile`, `RadioListTile` and `SwitchListTile` rows; `ListTile`; `CircularProgress` and `LinearProgress`; `Slider` (one or two thumbs, divisions, marks, vertical); `Card`, `Chip`, `Badge` and `Separator`; `Field` with `TextField` and `TextArea`; `Tooltip`, `Dialog`, `AlertDialog`, `Menu` (with check box and radio items and submenus) and `Select`; and `ScrollArea`. Themes now also set `--oito2-color-selected-fill`, `--oito2-color-on-selected-fill`, `--oito2-color-on-primary-text` and `--oito2-color-off-thumb`, and `componentColors` returns them.
- beb2148: Add the data, display and icon components. `@oito2/ui` gets `ListBox` (`Root`, `Item`, `Group`, `GroupLabel`, `Empty`) over a `ListCollection` and a `SelectionController`, `TreeView` with `TreeController` (virtualized rows, tristate check boxes, filtering, inline rename with F2 and lazy loading of children), `JsonTreeView` with `jsonTree()`, `Avatar` and `AvatarGroup` with `initials()`, `Marquee`, `TimerController` with `TimerText` and `formatDuration()`, `AnimatedIcon` with `animatedIcons`, `PlaceholderIcon`, `DiagonalClip`, the `draggable` and `undershoot` attachments and the `pageTransition` transition. The new `@oito2/ui/icons` entry re-exports every Lucide icon and adds `freedesktopIcon()`, which maps freedesktop.org icon names to Lucide icons.
- 91f11ee: Fix focus and keyboard handling of nested overlays. A `Select`, `Menu`, `Popover` or other overlay opened from a `Dialog`, `Sheet`, `CommandPalette` or `Tour` keeps the focus instead of losing it to the trap of the panel below. Menus and selects opened by the pointer enter their items with the arrow keys, a submenu opened by hovering leaves the focus on its trigger and opens once the pointer rests on it, and Tab returns the focus to the trigger before moving on. A right click outside closes menus and popovers, and a second right click moves an open context menu. Closing panels stop handling Escape and focus at once instead of after their exit animation. `Dialog.Content` and `Sheet.Content` take `finalFocus`, the element focused on close when the trigger is gone. Shortcuts with punctuation (`Ctrl+-`, `Ctrl+,`) follow the keyboard layout instead of the key position. A disabled command of an inner `Commands` scope no longer overrides the outer command's shortcut. `CommandPalette` runs a command after it closes and plays its exit animation.
- 466f4c7: Add the oito2 foundation: design tokens (radii, spacing, sizes, accent, neutral and status colors, the Inter type scale) in TypeScript and as CSS custom properties (`@oito2/ui/styles.css`, `tokens.css`, `density.css`, `base.css`, `fonts.css`), WCAG contrast helpers, platform and desktop environment detection, window button layout parsers for GNOME, KDE and Xfce, system settings with `SystemScope`, English and Brazilian Portuguese strings with `LocaleProvider`, and locale-aware formatting of byte sizes, compact numbers and relative times.
- f94a055: Add the interaction infrastructure. Collections: `ListCollection` and `TreeCollection` (immutable, with navigation that skips disabled items, accent-insensitive search and filtering, grouping and tree edits), the reactive `SelectionController` (single or multiple selection with Shift ranges and Control/Command toggles) and `AsyncListController` (paged loading with filter, sort and cancellation). Shortcuts: `KeyChord` and `Shortcut` (`Shortcut.parse('Mod+Shift+P')`, sequences such as `G H`, platform labels such as `⇧⌘P`), the `Commands` scope component, `useCommands()` and `shortcutConflicts`. Attachments: `focusBorder`, a keyboard-only focus ring around a group, and `edgeFocus`, arrow-key focus movement that stops at the group's edges. `ThemeProvider` now also hosts the overlays opened inside it, so they keep its theme.
- c66bd4f: Add the layout and navigation components. `@oito2/ui` gets `BorderContainer`, `Section`, `Tile` and `TileList`, `Watermark`, `SelectableContainer`, `Banner`, `InfoBox` and `InfoBadge`, `Expandable` and `Accordion` (also exported as `ExpansionPanel`), `Tabs` in the pill and subtle styles, in a row or a column, with a `tabs` slot on `HeaderBar`, `NavigationRail` in three styles, `NavigationBar`, `NavigationPage`, which switches between a rail and a bottom bar by width and input, `MasterDetail`, `PanedView` and `Splitter` with keyboard-resizable dividers, `Steps`, `Pagination`, `PageIndicator`, `Carousel`, `TableOfContents` with scroll-spy and `PageController`. Every color theme now also defines the message colors (`--oito2-color-info-box-<type>`, `--oito2-color-info-badge-text-<type>`…) and `--oito2-color-on-primary-tint`, and the presets of `@oito2/ui-themes` include them.
- 614ca49: Add the overlays, pickers and shortcut components. `@oito2/ui` gets `Popover` with `PopoverController`, `HoverCard`, `ContextMenu` (with the `Menu` items), `Sheet` from either side or the bottom with snap points, `Toaster` with `toast()`, `toast.promise()` and `ToastHandle`, `FloatingPanel`, `Tour` with `TourController`, `Calendar` and `DatePicker` (single date or range, ISO week numbers), `ColorPicker` and `ColorPickerButton` (hex, RGB and HSL fields, opacity, swatches), `ShortcutLabel`, `ShortcutRecorder` and `CommandPalette` with `openCommandPalette()`. `DateEntry` gains a button that opens a calendar (`pickerButton`).
- d9c76ff: Make props and strings behave the same in every component. Attributes and event handlers a component doesn't name reach its main element: a consumer's handler runs before the component's own, and `preventDefault()` in it cancels the component's behavior (`IconButton`, `Menu.Trigger`, `Popover.Trigger`, `Sheet.Close`, `DateEntry`, `Rating`, `NavigationMenu` parts and others no longer drop or replace them); `ThemeProvider` keeps the consumer's `style` and `Chip` its attributes. Every component with a value or an open state takes `default<Name>` and reports changes through `on<Name>Change` (`PasswordField` gets `onValueChange`, `defaultVisible` and `onVisibleChange`, `Pagination` `defaultPageSize`); `Select.Root` gets `open`, `defaultOpen` and `onOpenChange`, and `Menu.Root` and `Tooltip.Root` get `defaultOpen`. The standard button, pager and date and time entry labels moved into `Localizations` (`back`, `ok`, `cancel`, `previousPage`, `dateHelp`, `today`, ...), so the `strings` of `LocaleProvider` replace them too; the search button and field read "Pesquisa" in Portuguese, as in Flutter. New strings name the color format chooser of `ColorPicker`, the `Steps` buttons, a `CodeBlock` without a `label` (now always a named region) and the zoom slider of `ImageCropper`. `Checkbox`, `Switch`, `RadioGroup` and `Slider` take `form` and submit nothing while disabled. An invalid `accent` falls back to the default one with a warning instead of failing to render, and `ColorDisk` shows an invalid color as an empty disk.
- ceb22f9: `Button` rendered as a link gets a `rel` prop, which defaults to `noopener` when `target` is `_blank`. `CodeBlock` gets `onLanguageDetected`, called with the language `autoDetect` picked (or `null`) each time the code is highlighted, so a page can show the detected language without highlighting the code a second time.
- 1de8210: `SystemSettings` gains `colorScheme` (`'light' | 'dark' | null`), the light or dark scheme the system asks for, matching `colorScheme` of oito2_ui's `Oito2SystemSettings`. `fallbackSystemSettings` and `composeSystemSettings` set it to `null` for now, and `sameSystemSettings` compares it.
- d61831d: Fix the Tauri integration and make the satellite packages share one copy of `@oito2/ui`. `@oito2/ui` becomes a peer dependency of every other package, and `@tauri-apps/api`, `@tauri-apps/plugin-dialog` and `@tauri-apps/plugin-fs` peer dependencies of `@oito2/ui-files`, `@oito2/ui-window` and `@oito2/ui-settings`, so the app picks their versions; the published packages no longer carry the test goldens. `@oito2/ui-files`: the Tauri drop target converts drag-drop positions to CSS pixels only on Windows, where wry reports physical pixels, so drops on HiDPI Linux and macOS land on the zone; a file whose size can't be read is refused with the new `unreadable` reason (`fileUnreadable` string) instead of failing the whole batch, the count limit is checked before the size, drag-and-drop and `FileUpload` failures are reported instead of left unhandled, previews follow `showPreviews`, the drop zone's accessible name no longer repeats the "Choose files" badge, MIME-only `accept` groups filter the native chooser through the extension table (`extensionsForMimeType`) and the table covers more types. `@oito2/ui-window`: `initWindow` takes a `signal`, returns the live controller when called again for the same window and rejects with a clear message without the plugin; `dispose()` clears the `data-oito2-*` attributes; a focus change no longer discards a state read in flight; `showMenu()` without a position opens under the pointer; `CommandError` and `isCommandError` type the plugin rejections. `@oito2/ui-settings`: `isRawSettings` validates the plugin payload, failed reads warn once instead of failing silently, and `CommandError` types the rejections. `@oito2/ui-themes`: `presetSchemeName(preset, { highContrast })` returns the `data-oito2-scheme` value of a preset. Rust: `set_decoration_mode` and `show_window_menu` wait for the main-thread work and reject with the real error, decoration modes switch cleanly on Linux (a visible window is refused), destroyed windows are forgotten, a relative `XDG_CONFIG_HOME` is ignored and the settings plugin's error kinds are `unsupported` and `internal`.
- fed95a0: Add the window chrome. `@oito2/ui` gets `TitleBar`, `WindowTitleBar` (set up for the desktop from the closest `SystemScope`: the GNOME, KDE Breeze, Xfwm, COSMIC or Windows window controls in the desktop's button order, room for the macOS traffic lights, no controls in a browser), `DialogTitleBar`, `HeaderBar` with a built-in search field, `WindowControl` and `WindowControls` in seven styles, the `titleBarGestures` attachment (drag, double-click to maximize, window menu on the secondary button) and the `WindowController` interface with `WindowScope`. `@oito2/ui-window` adds `initWindow()`, `NativeWindowController` for Tauri windows, `WindowResizeHandles` for undecorated Linux windows and typed wrappers of the `tauri-plugin-oito2-window` commands, which now set and report the window frame and show the native window menu on Linux and Windows.
